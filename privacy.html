Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This site (alt10fd0f.github.io, "the site") hosts Kite Discord-bot tutorials written by ALT10fd0f ("I", "me"). It is a hobby project and is not officially affiliated with Kite. This policy explains what is collected when you use it. Browsing the tutorials needs no account; logging in with Discord is how the features that need to know who you are (favorites, difficulty ratings, support requests, the chat helper) work.

What is collected when you log in with Discord

You log in through Discord's own authorization screen and approve these permissions (scopes): identify, email, guilds and connections. After you approve, the site and my server receive:

  • Profile: your Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar and locale, whether you have 2FA and what Nitro type you have, and (from your ID) when your account was created.
  • Email address and whether Discord has verified it. You can switch off sharing it in your account panel; the site still receives it from Discord, but it is left out of the reports and support requests.
  • Server membership, used only for one check: the guilds permission lets me see the list of servers you are in. We only use the server connection to check whether you are in the Kite support server. The list itself is not saved, not shown to anyone and not included in any report; only the answer ("member" or "not a member") is used.
  • Connected accounts: the accounts linked to your Discord profile (for example the type and name of a linked service).

Each login sends a short report with this information to a private thread on a Discord server that I control, so I can see who is using the site. Your login creates (once) a thread for you; later logins are posted to the same thread.

Showcase submissions

If you use Submit showcase, the title, category, text and optional link you enter are sent to me (to a private Discord channel and/or a private automation I run), together with your username and ID and your email unless you turned that off. They are not saved on the site database. I use them to review your work and to contact you, and by submitting you allow me to publish it on this site if I choose to.

Support requests

When you send a support request, the subject, message and category are posted in a private staff channel on Discord with your username and ID (and your email unless you turned that off). The Kite Tutorials bot also sends you a copy by direct message and relays replies between you and staff. DMs only work if you allow them from the bot; the request is still received if they are closed.

Difficulty ratings and favorites

Your favorites are private to you. Your ratings are stored against your ID so you can change them; only the totals (average and number of ratings) are shown publicly.

Who else handles data

  • Discord (login, the reports and support messages).
  • GitHub Pages (hosts the website; may log visits as part of hosting).
  • Vercel (runs my server code and keeps routine request logs).
  • Upstash (the database).

I do not sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking cookies.

Your choices and your rights

  • See and edit: click your name at the top right to see what is stored and to change your display name and email-sharing choice.
  • Delete: use Request data deletion in the same panel. It erases your profile, favorites, ratings and support request records from the site database and logs you out. I am notified so I can also delete your Discord thread and support messages by hand.
  • Revoke access: in Discord, go to Settings → Authorized Apps and remove the app at any time.
  • You can contact me on Discord (link in the footer) with any request or question.

Retention

Data is kept until you delete it or I clean it up. Discord messages remain until deleted by me or by you via a deletion request.

Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in your country). The site is not meant for anyone younger.

Changes

I may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.

Official Staff Handbook & Operational Policy Manual

01. Introduction

Welcome to the official **Florida State Roleplay (FSRP)** Staff Team. As a member of our staff, you have been trusted with the responsibility of maintaining the integrity, immersion, and enjoyment of our community environment.

This handbook serves as your primary reference guide for administrative operations, code of conduct, and disciplinary standards. Compliance with the rules outlined herein is mandatory for all ranks.

Crucial Note: Staff discretion is an asset, but absolute adherence to the disciplinary matrix is required unless authorized by Senior Administration.

02. Core Values

Impartiality

Treat every community member equally, regardless of their tenure, status, or personal relationships.

Integrity

Uphold server regulations transparently, even when unmonitored. Model exemplary roleplay standard.

Professionalism

Maintain composure during high-stress moderation tickets. De-escalate conflicts effectively.

03. Code of Conduct

  • No Powergaming/Abuse of Power: Utilizing staff permissions (e.g., noclip, godmode, spawning menus) during active roleplay scenarios is strictly prohibited.
  • Confidentiality: Internal staff communications, updates, and disciplinary discussions are strictly confidential. Leakage results in an immediate blacklisted demotion.
  • Activity Thresholds: All staff members must log a minimum of 5 hours weekly on administrative duties unless an approved Leave of Absence (LOA) is submitted.

04. Chain of Command

Always direct issues to your immediate supervisor before escalating up the structural tier. Jumping the Chain of Command (CoC) will result in administrative reprimand.

Tier Level Rank Title Primary Jurisdiction
Tier 1 Trial Staff / Moderator In-game support tickets, basic reports
Tier 2 Administrator Ban processing, complex scene management
Tier 3 Staff Management Staff training, roster updates, appeals
Tier 4 Executive / Founder Core database modifications, structural changes

06. Case Handling Procedures

When handling player tickets, adhere strictly to the following 3-step dynamic:

    1. **Investigate:** Pull logs, spectate discreetly if ongoing, and gather clip evidence from both reporting and accused parties. 2. **Mediate:** Bring both parties to a secure administrative zone. Allow each side to present their perspective without interruption. 3. **Execute:** Render your decision calmly based strictly on facts and evidence, documenting the ticket in the discord logs database.

Modal / Form Block

In this showcase I will tell you on how to properly setup a modal/form block. Please follow step by step on what I have below.

Visit Showcase ↗
  • Create a new command.
  • Attach on a Show Modal block to the bottom of the command.
  • Click on the orange button that says configure modal.
  • Once in, add a title and click "Add input".
  • Add in an identifier. Something short and simple, Usually one word relating to the question. (This is how you'll get your answers) Can not have two of the same identifiers
  • Add a label, This will be the title of the question.
  • Add a placeholder, This will be whats inside the question.
  • Create all the questions you may need.
  • Add on a create response message.
  • Make it say completed or whatever you'd like.
  • Add on a create channel message block.
  • Have it send to a channel to collect responses in.
  • Type into the create channel message {{input('identifier')}}. But replace the word identifier with what you typed into the identifier box above.

And boom, There is how you create a modal/form block. If there are any questions about it please ping me in general inside the discord.
